Introduction

I aim to operate with both awareness and heart by constantly searching for opportunities such as pilots, partnerships, and student-support tools to improve the student experience for our fully online learners, while still staying tuned in to the individual experiences. I build connections across teams, with campus partners, facilitate conversations, and monitor long-term impact, all with a warm, encouraging tone. I am mission-driven and strategic, yet grounded in care and belonging. I find it important to hold space for others, celebrate their wins, and quietly remain focused on the bigger picture and its impacts on our learners and staff.

Context

This project will be accessible from this site with links to multimedia resources and supplemental content.

Target Learners

LSU Online students enrolled in fully online, accelerated courses at LSU.

Purpose

Continual improvement of the student experience.

Instructional Problem

Close the gap in navigating various LSU and LSU Online websites for online students.

Goals and Learning Outcomes

  • LSU Online students will be able to identify at least three key registration-related dates.
  • LSU Online students will be able to navigate to Workday Student to identify at least two sections of a required course and add one of them to their schedule.
  • Students will analyze their degree plan or recommendations and create a draft course plan for the upcoming term.
  • Students will complete an interactive quiz related to registration dates for LSU Online students.
  • Students will create a sample academic plan, save a schedule, and share the finished product.
